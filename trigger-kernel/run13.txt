attempt 13: a recorder that can only assign, and the initproc report

Two rounds died in "Build the kernel" with no compiler diagnostic in the job log (the step log is cut
to 55 lines; the build's own log stays on the runner), so the only thing to do is remove everything
that could plausibly fail to compile and keep the part that answers the question.

0005 is now four globals in bsd/vm/vm_unix.c and four assignments at the return of
__shared_region_map_and_slide: calls, file count, mapping count, and the kern_return_t dyld sees.
No VFS calls, no helper, no struct access.

0004 prints them at the initproc panic, with the stages it already had:

  DroidVM initproc: initproc failed to start: <dyld's exit reason> | sr=CALLS(fFILES/mMAPS)=RESULT
                    cryptex=5/0 addon3=1/2 owner=6/0 union=U

RESULT is the answer to "did the kernel refuse the dyld cache". 0 = the mapping succeeded and dyld
broke later (then it is memory, the 16 KiB slide, or the seed); EPERM(1) = the "not on the process
root's volume nor preboot volume" test; anything else names its own stage. union says whether the
owner chain's MNT_UNION actually took (rootvnode == the SystemOS mount), which is what decides whether
that test can even fire for a cache on the SystemOS volume.
